For women who’ve done it all—and are ready for what’s next. Stubborn & Sophisticated is a luxury brunch for women who are accomplished, capable, and quietly wondering if the next chapter should look different. It’s for those who are successful on paper but craving clarity, alignment, or simply space to breathe and reflect. Hosted by two powerhouse female entrepreneurs — Adriana L.
